# Why SRP SmartRecruit (not “just build in-house”)

Use this in a short meeting. Speak the **30-second answer** first. Use the slides only if they ask for more.

---

## 30-second answer (memorize this)

> You *can* build a CV form in-house. That’s not what SRP is.  
> SRP is a **full hiring operating system** — candidates, jobs, screening, interviews, offers, HR docs, roles, and audit — with AI that works on *your* talent data.  
> In-house means 12–18 months and a product team while hiring still hurts.  
> SRP means you start **now**. You’re buying **speed and lower risk**, not another spreadsheet with a chatbot.

**One-liner if interrupted:**  
> In-house builds a tool. SRP delivers a hiring system — so your team fills roles instead of building software.

---

## Slide 1 — The question

**“Why not build this ourselves?”**

Honest answer: you can build *parts*.  
What takes years is the **whole recruitment OS** — workflow + AI + security + multi-team use — working every day.

---

## Slide 2 — What in-house usually becomes

| In-house path | What you get |
|---|---|
| Forms + shared drive | Chaos at volume |
| Generic ChatGPT paste | No proof, no process, data risk |
| One developer’s side project | Breaks when they leave |
| “We’ll add AI later” | Still manual screening for 12+ months |

**Cost of delay:** open roles stay open while you invent software.

---

## Slide 3 — What SRP already is

**One system for the full funnel**

1. Source & store candidates  
2. AI screen against the job (with reasons on record)  
3. Match internal talent (not only new CVs)  
4. Interviews → offers → joining / HR  
5. Roles, audit, workspace isolation  

**Special in plain words:** AI that cites *your* data — not a black-box chatbot.

---

## Slide 4 — Why SRP wins on time & risk

| | Build in-house | Use SRP |
|---|---|---|
| Time to value | 12–18+ months | Days / weeks |
| Product + security team | You hire & keep | Already built |
| AI + workflow | You invent both | Shipped together |
| Updates | Your backlog | Continuous product |

You’re not paying for “code.”  
You’re paying to **stop losing candidates and calendar time**.

---

## Slide 5 — Close

**Ask them:**

> “Do we want to be a software company — or a company that hires faster?”

**Your close line:**

> SRP is the recruitment OS for teams who need results this quarter — not a roadmap for next year.
